Human RNome Project — NA12878 Whole-Genome Sequencing DNA Variants

Whole-genome sequencing variant calls for sample NA12878, which was utilized throughout the Human RNome Project. Variants were called independently using both DeepVariant (v1.9.0) and GATK GenotypeGVCFs. Callsets were normalized, filtered for high-quality variants (minimum genotype quality 20, depth 10, allele balance 0.2), and intersected to retain only variants identified by both callers, resulting in a high-confidence variant set.
