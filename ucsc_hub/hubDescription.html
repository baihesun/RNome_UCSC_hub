Human rRNA Modifications

RNA modification tracks for human rRNA sequences (18S, 28S, 5.8S, 5S) derived from three orthogonal sequencing technologies.

Track colors

Colors encode modification type (hue) and stoichiometry (saturation). Vivid = highly modified; pale = partially modified.

Score

Detection confidence rescaled to 0–1000 per file independently. Darker = higher confidence. Scores are not comparable across tracks.
