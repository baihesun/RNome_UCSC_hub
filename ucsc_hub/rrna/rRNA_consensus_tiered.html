rRNA tiered consensus

This concensus track represents RNA modification sites aggregated from sites detected by short-read sequencing (Illumina), long-read sequencing (ONT), and mass spectrometry technologies. The sites are grouped into two confidence tiers: tier 1 reflects the strongest evidence from optimized cross-platform comparisons, and tier 2 comes from agreement between two or more raw datasets. Together they provide a ranked reference atlas of RNA modifications, with higher tiers representing sites supported by more independent lines of evidence.

Display Conventions and Configuration

Items are colored by modification type; within each modification type, color saturation encodes confidence tier — tier1 sites (strongest cross-platform evidence) are vivid, tier2 sites (supported by ≥2 datasets) are pale. Modification types not shown in the color key below are rendered in gray (#808080).
