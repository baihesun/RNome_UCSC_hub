Human RNome Project — tRNA mass spectrometry

This track catalogues modifications identified via oligonucleotide mass spectrometry across all submitted samples. Entries meet the following minimum quality thresholds: a false discovery rate (FDR) ≤ 5% for the detected oligonucleotide, a modification level ≥ 10%, and concordant detection across all samples covering the respective position.
