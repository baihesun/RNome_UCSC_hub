tRNA MS

This track catalogues modifications identified via oligonucleotide mass spectrometry across all submitted samples. Entries meet the following minimum quality thresholds: a false discovery rate (FDR) ≤ 5% for the detected oligonucleotide, a modification level ≥ 10%, and concordant detection across all samples covering the respective position.

Display Conventions and Configuration

Items are colored by modification type; within each modification type, color saturation is proportional to modification frequency — vivid colors mark high-frequency (high-stoichiometry) sites, pale colors mark low-frequency sites. Modification types not shown in the color key below are rendered in gray (#808080).
